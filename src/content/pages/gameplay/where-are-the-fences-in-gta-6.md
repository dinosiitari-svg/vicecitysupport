  - q: "How do you sell stolen goods in GTA 6?"
    a: "Open the index, go to Robberies and look at the goods drop-offs. Only fences you've unlocked are listed. Highlight one to see what it will take from what you're carrying and how good its price is, from Very Low to Very High."
  - q: "What can you sell to a fence?"
    a: "Narcotics, jewelry and marked bills. Each fence pays best for one of those, so check before you drive over. Cars go to Moses, Randy or a Pay 'n' Spray instead."
