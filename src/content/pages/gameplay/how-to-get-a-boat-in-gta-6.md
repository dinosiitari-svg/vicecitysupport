Smaller boats and speedboats sit at marinas around Vice City, and launch-week guides say bigger boats and yachts can be stolen too. Nobody has found a way to buy, register or store a boat yet, so a boat you steal isn't kept."

    a: "No. Garages take cars only, and boats can't be registered or delivered. A stolen boat isn't yours, so it won't be waiting where you left it."

    a: "Guides say it returns to his dock. One says you get a text that it's being repaired, and that a save and reload brings it back if it's missing. Another says a broken boat engine can't be repaired on the spot."

- Whether Jason's jet ski starts on a trailer at his house.
- Whether Jason's fishing boat stays available after the story moves him out of Key Lento. One guide says it goes away.
- Coast Guard or police boats chasing you at sea. None of the guides covers it.
- Exact marina spots beyond the Ocean Beach marina already on our map.
