## Trophies worth planning for early

- **Positive Mental Attitude**: do every meditation spot as you find it, starting with Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1. See [all meditation spot locations](/collectibles/all-meditation-spot-locations/).
- **Top Fun**: the 14 drone race locations. See [all drone race locations](/collectibles/all-drone-race-locations/).
- **The Shootist**: gold at all 42 shooting range challenges. See [how shooting ranges work](/gameplay/how-do-shooting-ranges-work-in-gta-6/).
- **Pest Control**: rob one Illegal Operation from every gang. See [how gang Illegal Operations work](/gameplay/how-do-gang-illegal-operations-work-in-gta-6/).
- **Cunning Stunts**: 250 stunts. See [how stunt jumps work](/gameplay/how-do-stunt-jumps-work-in-gta-6/).
- **Hot Together**: escape a four-star wanted level with your partner. See [how the wanted system works](/gameplay/how-does-the-wanted-system-work-in-gta-6/).

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The grade split (2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze) comes from one guide only.
- Whether Lucia's Chapter 1 meditation really is gone for good if you skip it.
- Exact conditions for several hidden trophies. Guides still disagree or only give hints for some of them.
- Guides disagree on wording for a few trophies, for example whether Cunning Stunts needs 250 *unique* stunts.
