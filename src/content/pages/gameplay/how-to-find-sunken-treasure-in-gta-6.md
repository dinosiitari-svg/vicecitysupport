## What isn't confirmed yet

- **Where the boat is.** GTA BOOM says the boat sits at the Key Lento charter dock; Item Level says to take the boat at your house.
- **Kenton's surname.** Spelled Rees by SlashGear and Reese by GTA BOOM.
- **Del Ocio's count.** Guides count 10 gold doubloons, but Item Level describes only four places to dig (three outside the hull, one inside). One dig can hold several coins, so both may be right.
- Most treasure prices, and whether more wrecks open later. GTA BOOM mentions a second batch of three wrecks from Kenton that we haven't checked yet.
