## What isn't confirmed yet

- **The exact trigger.** Game Rant and Tech4Gamers say the lock-in starts with Tourist Trap when you pick Lucia up from work. TheGamer describes it as the moment you accept Mattia's job at the yacht party. Either way, treat the call from Kat and Mattia as your warning.
- **The $5,000 limit.** Guides say Family Savings caps at $5,000 during Chapter 1, but we haven't tested whether any other money survives.
- **Which other stashes are lost.** TheGamer says some other stash spots are lost too, but doesn't name them.
