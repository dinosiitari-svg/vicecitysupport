question: "How to fix your car for free in GTA 6"
answer: "In Chapter 1, call Joey from Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento: open your phone, go to Contacts and ring him, and he repairs your car for nothing and leaves it parked next to you. The story mission Just a Scratch also gives you a free full engine repair and respray. After Chapter 1, free repairs come from the three daily uses that Scooter Bros Premium includes. Otherwise you pay at a gas pump, a Pay 'n' Spray or a mod shop."

items:
  - title: "Play Just a Scratch (Chapter 1)"
    detail: "This short story mission unlocks after Gone Fishing. Drive the '70 Ganado from Jason's house to Joey outside Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, talk to him (hold up on the d-pad and press X / A), drive into the garage and pick Full Engine. It costs nothing. Then choose Cosmetics > Respray for a free paint job."
  - title: "Call Joey for a free repair"
    detail: "After that mission Joey's number is in your phone. Press up on the d-pad, open Contacts and call him. After a short loading screen your car is back, fixed and parked next to you. GamesRadar+ says this only works in Chapter 1; Game Rant doesn't mention a limit."
  - title: "Use Scooter Bros Premium's free daily uses"
    detail: "The Scooter Bros app delivers your owned car to you and can repair it anywhere, even in the middle of a chase. The Premium upgrade, a one-off purchase of about $12,000 to $12,500, gives three free uses per in-game day. A use covers a delivery with a repair, collecting a car from the impound, or replacing a destroyed car."
  - title: "Let the story fix it"
    detail: "Some story moments repair your car for you automatically. GamesRadar+ says this is rare, so don't count on it."
  - title: "Reload a save if the car is wrecked early on"
    detail: "Before Just a Scratch, if the Ganado gets destroyed, load your last save (Pause Menu > Game > Save/Load Game). You come back at Jason's house with the car outside."
faq:
  - q: "How much does a car repair cost in GTA 6?"
    a: "It depends on the damage. Game Rant paid over $700 for one engine repair at a gas pump, and it still didn't fully fix the car. Cultured Vultures puts a typical repair on a good car at about $1,000. Pay 'n' Spray and mod shop prices haven't been published yet."
  - q: "Where can I pay to repair my car?"
    a: "At a gas pump, a Pay 'n' Spray or a mod shop. Pull in, choose Services, and Repair is the first option. To find the nearest one, open the Index on your phone and look under Vehicles, which sorts places by distance."
  - q: "Is a gas station repair a full repair?"
    a: "Reportedly not. GamesRadar+ says a gas pump doesn't restore the engine to 100% and can't fix a critical failure. It's handy because you can refuel at the same stop."
  - q: "Is Scooter Bros Premium worth it for repairs?"
    a: "If you drive hard, probably. At about $1,000 a repair, Premium pays for itself after roughly a dozen repairs, and you get three free uses every in-game day after that."
  - q: "How do I check how damaged my car is?"
    a: "Press down on the d-pad to show the engine meter above the minimap. Green is fine, orange is moderate damage, red is critical, and a red cross means the car is wrecked. Bad engine damage can start a fire, and damaged tires or axles pull the steering."
  - q: "Can you fix bikes, boats or planes for free?"
    a: "None of the guides we've read cover it. Pumps reportedly repair motorbikes for a fee. Jason's fishing boat comes back to his dock after it sinks."

## Free vs paid repairs at a glance

| Method | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|
| Just a Scratch mission | Free (engine + respray) | Chapter 1, after Gone Fishing |
| Call Joey | Free | Chapter 1 (possibly longer) |
| Scooter Bros Premium | 3 free uses a day after buying Premium | Any time you own Premium |
| Gas pump | Varies, $700+ reported for one engine repair | Any time |
| Pay 'n' Spray / mod shop | Not published yet | Any time |
