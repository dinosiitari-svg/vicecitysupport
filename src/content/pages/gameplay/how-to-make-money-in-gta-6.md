## Three kinds of money

GTA 6 separates your money into three pots, and knowing the difference saves you losing a big score:

- **Bank balance**: safe. This is what you spend from and what you keep if things go wrong.
- **Cash on you**: reportedly capped at $20,000 and lost if you die. Get it to an ATM.
- **Loot in a bag**: stolen goods aren't money until a fence buys them.
