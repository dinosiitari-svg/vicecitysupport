answer: "You can buy body armor at gun stores or take it for free from police patrol car trunks. One guide says the first gun shop you visit in the story, in Key Lento, has vests on the right-hand wall, and another lists armor at $250. To put a vest on, hold L1 / LB to open the item wheel and equip it from there. A blue bar then appears above your health and soaks up bullet damage until it's gone. A damaged vest can't be repaired, so you'll need a new one."
