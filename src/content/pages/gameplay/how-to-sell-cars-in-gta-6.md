question: "How to sell cars in GTA 6"
answer: "Sell your own registered cars at any Pay 'n' Spray, which you unlock in the Chapter 1 mission 'Just a Scratch'. Stolen cars go to a fence instead. Fences pay more for cars in good condition, and some won't take a car that's too hot or still has a tracker fitted. Scan a car with the WAiNK app first to see what it's worth and which fences want it."
