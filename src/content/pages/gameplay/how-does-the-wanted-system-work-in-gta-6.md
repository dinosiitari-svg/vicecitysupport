## What's new compared with GTA V

- **Crimes need a witness.** In GTA V the stars appeared the moment you did something wrong. In GTA 6, someone has to see it and report it, an alarm has to trigger, or a stolen car's tracker has to give you away.
- **Police work from a description.** They build a picture of you from witnesses and cameras: what you're wearing, what you're driving, what you're carrying and whether you're a pair. Icons under the stars show what they currently know.
- **Areas remember.** Crime hot spots stay hot for a while, a bit like bounties in Red Dead Redemption 2.

## What isn't confirmed yet

Exactly which crimes give which star level, how long a hot area lasts, and whether there's a long-term record that follows you between crimes. We'll fill these in as we test them.
