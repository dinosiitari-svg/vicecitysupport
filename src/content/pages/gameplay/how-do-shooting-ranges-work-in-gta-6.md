## Quick tips

- On the timed Griffin courses, slow motion helps with the small spinning targets.
- At Rodgers Ranch, one body hit then a headshot keeps the multiplier climbing. Don't spray: misses cost you.
- A shotgun is the reported pick for headshots in the zombie event.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- Whether 100% completion needs all golds or just a bronze in each challenge.
- Exact positions of the two ranges. They're described by area only, so they're not on our map yet.
