## Why studying animals matters

The Animals log is one of the checklists behind 100% completion, so it's worth studying anything new you pass while free roaming. Hunting also starts with a study: it tells you the best weapon to use on that animal.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- Where the binoculars come from and whether either character has them from the start.
- The exact button for looking through the binoculars (only one guide mentions L2/LT).
- Whether phone photos count.
- Whether the Hunting tab fills only by killing or by studying too.
- One guide points to a zoo in Vice-Dale County for caged animals, but hasn't pinned down where it is, so it isn't on our map.
