question: "How do you fight in GTA 6? (melee controls)"
answer: "On the default controls, punch with Square (X on Xbox), hold it for a heavy attack, hold L2 (LT) to lock on, hold Cross (A) to block and hold R2 (RT) to grab. To counter, press Cross (A) just before a hit lands, then strike with Square (X). From a grab you can throw, choke an enemy until they pass out, or sneak up behind someone for a stealth takedown. Jason and Lucia also have a few moves of their own, like Jason's dropkick and suplex and Lucia's flying kick and triangle choke."

items:
  - title: "Lock on: hold L2 / LT"
    detail: "Locks you onto one opponent so you circle them instead of swinging at the air."
  - title: "Punch: Square / X"
    detail: "Your basic strike. Hold the button instead of tapping it for a slower, heavier hit."
  - title: "Block: hold Cross / A"
    detail: "Both guides put block on Cross (A). GamerBlurb says a quick tap at the right moment dodges instead."
  - title: "Counter: Cross / A just before the hit, then Square / X"
    detail: "Time the defensive press just before the enemy's attack connects, then hit back straight away. Dexerto calls the first press a block, GamerBlurb calls it a dodge, but the timing and the follow-up are the same."
  - title: "Grab: hold R2 / RT"
    detail: "From a grab, press Square (X) to hit them, or hold it to put them in a vulnerable position. To throw them, keep holding R2 (RT), flick the left stick and press R2 (RT) again."
  - title: "Chokehold: hold R2 / RT, hold Square / X, then mash it"
    detail: "Dexerto says this keeps going until the enemy passes out, so it's the quiet way to put someone down."
  - title: "Stealth takedown: from behind, hold R2 / RT and mash Square / X"
    detail: "Sneak up behind an enemy who hasn't spotted you first."
  - title: "Running moves: spear, dropkick and flying kick"
    detail: "Spear: sprint (click L3 / left stick) and hold Square (X) as you reach them. Dropkick (Jason) or flying kick (Lucia): run by holding L3 without clicking it, then press Square (X)."
  - title: "Jason's German suplex"
    detail: "Grab from behind with R2 (RT), hold Square (X) briefly and let go, pull the left stick back, then press Square (X)."
  - title: "Lucia's flying triangle choke"
    detail: "Hold R2 (RT) from the front, pull the left stick straight down, tap Square (X), then mash it."
