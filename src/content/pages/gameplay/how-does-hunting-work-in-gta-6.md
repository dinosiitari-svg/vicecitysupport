question: "How does hunting work in GTA 6?"
answer: "Hunting opens in Chapter 2 when a hunter called Bret texts Jason after the mission Coffee Break and starts the side mission Veteran Hunted. After that, Bret hands out 15 Target Decks, each a short list of species to bring him, shown as purple jobs on the map. Study animals to log them, kill them with one clean shot to the head or heart for a Pristine carcass, and sell what you bring back at Bret's shop in Mount Kalaga National Park. Lucia can't start it."

listTitle: "How to start hunting and get paid"
items:
  - title: "Unlock it with Bret (Chapter 2, Jason only)"
    detail: "Bret Vogel texts Jason after the mission Coffee Break and starts the side mission Veteran Hunted. Lucia can't take it. One guide adds Breakfast Run as a requirement; the others don't."
  - title: "Finish Veteran Hunted"
    detail: "You get Bret's Killtelligence SM-69 rifle (two guides add a $500 payment). If you lose the rifle you can take it back out of an Ammu-Nation weapons locker. Selling to Bret opens after this mission."
  - title: "Pick up a Target Deck"
    detail: "There are 15 Target Decks, each with two or three species. They show as purple hunting jobs on the map, and finishing one makes Bret text you the next. Deck 1 is a whitetail buck and doe."
  - title: "Study the animal first"
    detail: "Aim at an animal and press R1 (LB on Xbox) to study it. That logs it in Progress > Animals and shows the best weapon, its habitat and what it sells for. Studying counts even if you don't kill it."
  - title: "Track it with Focus"
    detail: "Press L3 + R3 (LS + RS on Xbox) to use Focus. Tracks show up and change as you get closer, and Focus also highlights the animal's vital spots. Stay at range, because animals spook."
  - title: "Take one clean shot"
    detail: "One shot to the head or heart gives a Pristine carcass. Every extra shot lowers the quality. Use an SM-69 rifle for deer, boar and alligators, a Wortham W-22 for small game like turkeys, skunks and rabbits, and a Hawk & Little Mediator for medium animals such as coyotes."
  - title: "Get the carcass to Bret"
    detail: "Carry small animals yourself and load them into a pickup bed (a Declasse Yosemite works). For heavy animals like alligators and bears, guides say you photograph the body and send it to Bret, who collects it and pays into your account."
  - title: "Sell at Killtelligence Provisions"
    detail: "Bret's shop is in Mount Kalaga National Park, Lummox County, and shows a dollar-sign marker on the map. Hover over the marker to see what your haul is worth before you drive over."
faq:
  - q: "How many Target Decks are there?"
    a: "Fifteen. All four launch guides agree on that number. Only the first three have been listed in full so far: whitetail buck and doe; wild turkey, spotted skunk and wild boar; then cane toad, nutria and Nile monitor."
  - q: "How much do animals sell for?"
    a: "Only a few prices have been reported. Whitetail does go for about $220 to $380 and bucks for about $290 to $505, depending on condition. A Canada goose is about $200 to $355. A single report puts a wild boar at $2,500. Unique animals pay much more, reportedly several thousand dollars each."
  - q: "Does Bret buy damaged carcasses?"
    a: "Sources disagree. One guide says he only takes Pristine or Good. Others say a Poor carcass still sells, just for less. Aim for one clean shot and you won't have to find out."
  - q: "Are there legendary animals?"
    a: "Yes, in some form. The survival challenge Legendary Hunter asks you to kill all 8 unique animals. One guide instead counts 11 special targets: 9 that only appear after Bret sends you a clue, plus 2 secret ones with no clue. Nobody has published a confirmed full list yet."
  - q: "Do you need a hunting licence?"
    a: "No guide mentions one. Bret's mission is the only gate reported so far."
  - q: "Does hunting count toward 100% completion?"
    a: "Reportedly yes: all 15 Target Decks plus studying 100 animals. The Animals list in the Progress menu has 255 entries, so you don't need all of them for 100%."
  - q: "Are there hunting trophies?"
    a: "The bronze trophy Fair Game is for hunting a unique animal for Bret. Guides don't agree yet on which animal unlocks it. Hunting also counts toward Master of None, for trying each activity type once."

## Survival challenges linked to hunting

| Challenge | What you do |
|---|---|
| Zoologist | Study 20 species |
| Alligator Kills | Kill 3 wild alligators |
| Hunting Spree | Kill a boar, a rattlesnake and a panther within 15 minutes without taking damage |
| Legendary Hunter | Kill all 8 unique animals |

These names come from one launch-week guide. We'll confirm them in-game.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The species in Target Decks 4 to 15.
- How many unique or legendary animals there are (8 or 11) and how their clues arrive.
- Whether Bret refuses Poor carcasses, and how much more a Pristine one pays.
- Whether small carcasses can go in a car trunk as well as a pickup bed.
- How many heavy animals Bret will collect for you at once.
- Prices for most animals.
