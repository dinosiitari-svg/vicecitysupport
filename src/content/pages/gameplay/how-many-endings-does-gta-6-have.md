- The exact number of endings. Sources list anywhere from three to "well over two" main versions plus small variations.
- Whether helping side characters or finishing Chapter 5's to-do list changes the ending, or only the credits.
- How much money counts as "enough" savings.
- Whether the relationship really locks during Chapter 5, or can still change later.
