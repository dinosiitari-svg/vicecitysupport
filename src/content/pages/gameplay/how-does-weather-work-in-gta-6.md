Leonida gets sunny spells, wind, rain, thunderstorms and morning mist, and hurricanes unlock later through a side questline. Rain often seems rare because at launch the weather could get stuck on clear for hours. Rockstar's 23 November update fixed that bug, but some players still report long dry stretches. You can't pick the weather during the story. Your options are to drive toward a storm or sleep to move the clock on. After the story, a Cycle Weather cheat lets you switch it, but cheats turn off saving."
