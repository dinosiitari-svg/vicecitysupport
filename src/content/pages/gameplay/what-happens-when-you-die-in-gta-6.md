## Why it matters

Respawning nearby means a failed shootout or crash costs you much less time getting back to what you were doing. Losing carried cash is the real penalty, so bank big payouts before taking risks. If you've just picked up a good gun from an enemy, it's gone if you die, so buy your own version when you can.
