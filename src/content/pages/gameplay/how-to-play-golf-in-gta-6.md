answer: "Yes. GTA 6 has two full golf courses, Leaf Links in Vice Beach and the Mount Hailes Country Club in Leonard County, plus three mini golf courses: King Gator Adventure Golf, Frontier Adventure Golf and Dino Golf in Frogswater. At a golf club, go to the nearest marked starting spot to begin. Mini golf holes are played in order and the fewest strokes wins. Only mini golf counts toward 100%, and you need at least two of the three courses."
