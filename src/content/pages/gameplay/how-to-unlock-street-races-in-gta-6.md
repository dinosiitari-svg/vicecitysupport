## Tips for your first races

- Use nitrous on straights, not in corners.
- Of the two rentals reported for the first race, the Dinka Sugoi accelerates better and the Vapid Dominator has the higher top speed.
- Second place is enough to move Yago's story on, so you don't have to restart for a win, but only first place pays the full prize.
- Bumper to Bumper ends with a wanted level. Get out of sight to lose the cops, and see our [wanted system guide](/gameplay/how-does-the-wanted-system-work-in-gta-6/).

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The exact race count (39 or 40) and whether Vice City street races count toward the trophy.
- Buy-ins and payouts, which differ between guides.
- Exact start points. Race starts are described by area only, so they're not on our map yet.
