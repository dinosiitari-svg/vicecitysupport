## Where sources disagree

- **Rank names:** four ranks plus a separate "Shattered" state in one preview, five ranks ending in "Irredeemable" in another.
- **Confirmation:** one outlet says Rockstar confirmed the permanent state; another says it comes from a single preview account and Rockstar hasn't detailed it.
- **Scope:** it's unclear whether a shattered profile affects one lead or both.
