question: "How to remove an advanced tracker in GTA 6"
answer: "You can't remove an advanced tracker until you've finished Randy's rare car questline. In Chapter 3 Randy gives you his Wish List. Deliver the cars on it, ending with the Declasse Mamba GT, and some time later Randy calls to say Pay 'n' Spray will now handle advanced trackers. After that, drive the car to any Pay 'n' Spray without slowing down, respray it and pay the registration fee. The tracker is removed and the car is yours, as long as you have a free garage slot."
