## Why there's a dog on a jet ski

It's a nod to a real Florida dog that became famous online for riding a jet ski in goggles and a life vest. In the game he's a rescue service for anyone stranded out at sea.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- Whether the rescue or the wallet can be repeated.
- The exact order of events. One guide has you throw the ball straight after the rescue; another says it happens on the island.
- Whether it works for both Jason and Lucia. The first-hand report shows Jason.
- An exact spot for Dog Island, so it isn't on our map yet.
