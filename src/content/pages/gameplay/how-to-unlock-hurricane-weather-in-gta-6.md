question: "How do you unlock hurricanes in GTA 6?"
answer: "Hurricane weather unlocks when you finish Freddie's Strangers and Freaks questline. Break into his doomsday compound in North Leonard County once, wait for his text, then break in again for the hidden mission Retest Freddie's Defenses. At the end a hurricane warning appears: choose Leave and the storm hits right away, or Stay and it arrives a few in-game days later. Either way, hurricanes then turn up in free roam at random."

  - title: "Find Freddie's compound"

    detail: "The intercom turns you away. Use the bulldozer to shift an orange shipping container, which hides a tunnel. Inside, avoid the proximity mines and shoot down two explosive drones. Freddie is then added to your contacts."

    detail: "The next part only opens once Freddie texts you, which can take several in-game days. KeenGamer warns that just sleeping over and over at a safehouse may not trigger it, so keep playing in the meantime."

    detail: "The tunnel is sealed this time. Climb in from the water tower, or go over the container and fence. Destroy five explosive drones (two, then three), follow Freddie through the bunker, sit in on his podcast, then choose Turn In or Fight."
  - title: "Choose Stay or Leave at the hurricane warning"
    detail: "Both choices finish the mission and unlock hurricane weather. Leave brings the storm straight away. Stay means waiting a few in-game days for it."
