question: "What should you know before you start GTA 6? Beginner tips"
answer: "Eat and drink before trouble starts (it tops up health, stamina and Focus), use your car's trunk as a wardrobe and gun locker, scan stolen cars for trackers with WAINK, fix cars cheaply at gas pumps, and hold down on the D-pad to see nearby activities without opening the map. Most of these systems aren't explained up front."

listTitle: "Spoiler-free tips for your first hours"
items:
  - title: "Eat and drink before a fight"
    detail: "Food and drinks refill health, stamina and the Focus meter, so you take hits better, sprint longer and get more slow-motion aiming. You can keep moving, driving or even shooting while you eat."
  - title: "Use Focus to read the area"
    detail: "Click both sticks to switch Focus on. It points you to the source of shouting or noises (often a random event), highlights enemies that are moving or making noise, and shows doors wired to alarms in red."
  - title: "Don't kick in red doors"
    detail: "Doors that glow red in Focus will set off an alarm. Find another way in, or come back once you have a lockpick."
  - title: "Treat your trunk as a wardrobe and armory"
    detail: "Your personal car's trunk holds spare guns and outfits. Driving off shuts it; hold left on the D-pad for the vehicle menu to open or close it and switch off the headlights."
  - title: "Change clothes to shake the police"
    detail: "Swapping outfits removes the clothing description police are working from. Any shop with a changing room lets you build and save outfits, and you can name them from a preset word list."
  - title: "Check stolen cars for a tracker"
    detail: "A red tracker icon in the bottom-left corner, next to the fuel and engine icons, means police can trace the car if you crawl along for too long. Hold up on the D-pad to open WAINK and scan a car before you take it. Getting a tracker removed is expensive."
  - title: "Repair at a gas pump"
    detail: "Any pump can fix a car or motorbike for a small fee, which is usually cheaper than having the Scooter Bros app repair it."
  - title: "Use Scooter Bros for your vehicles"
    detail: "The app brings your owned vehicles to you, repairs them and gets them out of the impound, all for a fee. A $12,500 Premium tier gives three free uses per in-game day."
  - title: "Hold down on the D-pad instead of opening the map"
    detail: "Time slows and nearby activities and missions appear. Mark one to get GPS directions straight away."
  - title: "Fast travel with Ryde Me"
    detail: "Call a ride, then aim at the driver (hold the left trigger) to get an option to skip the journey for $100."
  - title: "Calm down a cop before it escalates"
    detail: "Aim at an officer with no gun drawn and you get options to defuse or provoke. Defusing can clear a one-star wanted level, though some officers will attack anyway."
  - title: "Look for shipwrecks early"
    detail: "Wrecks can be explored early and hold valuable loot, which makes diving one of the easiest early money sources. Watch out for alligators in any water."
  - title: "Feel for the vibration"
    detail: "A strong rumble while you're on foot means something nearby can be scanned. Open WAINK to find it."
  - title: "Carry a Zombix"
    detail: "Zombix is the healing item for combat. You'll find some lying around, and shop clerks sell it if you ask, since it usually isn't on the shelves."
  - title: "Stock up at convenience stores"
    detail: "Guides count 72 convenience stores (chains like 247, LTD Food Mart and Xero Food Mart, plus independents). They sell Zombix, pet food and groceries for your fridge at home. Filter the map with Food & Drink > Convenience Stores to list them."
  - title: "Hide early cash under Jason's floorboards"
    detail: "Until the Buck Me mission sets up a bank account, one guide says loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom are a safe place to stash cash, since dying costs you what you're carrying."
  - title: "Earn good-behaviour points"
    detail: "Petting dogs and cats (aim at them with the left trigger) and putting litter in a bin raise Focus and add good-behaviour points to your criminal profile."
  - title: "Shop as a couple"
    detail: "With both Jason and Lucia in a store you can buy and try on clothes for either one, and buying for your partner improves their relationship."
  - title: "Buy garages for storage, not as hangouts"
    detail: "Garages around the map add slots for cars and motorbikes. That's all they're for."
  - title: "Visit more than one clothes shop and barber"
    detail: "Most of them stock items you can't get anywhere else. At a safehouse mirror you can only trim your hair or beard, not restyle it."
  - title: "Push stuck boats and flipped cars yourself"
    detail: "Walk into a vehicle until a prompt appears, then hold Square to shove it. It's enough to get a beached boat back in the water or tip a car back onto its wheels, so you don't have to pay to get it moved."
  - title: "Hit people with your car door"
    detail: "Press Square while driving to swing the door open into someone. On a motorbike it's a kick instead, handy for knocking a rival off their bike in a race."
  - title: "Find the extra car controls"
    detail: "Hold left on the D-pad to bring up the radio list, then push the left stick up for the vehicle menu: hazard lights, full beams, windows and, on convertibles, the roof. Tap left or right on the D-pad to indicate."
  - title: "Swim faster and stay under longer"
    detail: "On the surface, click the left stick once to swim faster and twice for top speed. That drains stamina, but once you dive the meter switches to lung capacity, so swimming underwater lets you keep full speed for roughly twice as long."
  - title: "Case a robbery before you pull it"
    detail: "Walk in as a normal customer, lock on to a guard and press right on the D-pad to tag them with an arrow, so you always know where they are once things kick off."
  - title: "Read the building colours on the map"
    detail: "Dark blue buildings have somewhere you can go inside, even if it's only a garage or a corridor. Light blue ones can't be entered. Look for the interaction prompt near the doors."
  - title: "Any explosion opens a safe"
    detail: "You don't need the code or a sticky bomb. A grenade, a rocket or a propane tank going off next to a safe will open it, though you may lose some of what's inside."

  - q: "How do you find random events in GTA 6?"
    a: "Listen for shouting or loud noises, then turn on Focus (click both sticks) to see where it's coming from."
  - q: "How do you lose a one-star wanted level quickly?"
    a: "Aim at a nearby officer who hasn't drawn a gun and pick the defuse option, or change into a different outfit. Neither works every time."
  - q: "How do you flip a car back over in GTA 6?"
    a: "Walk up to it until a push prompt appears, then hold Square (on PlayStation) to shove it back onto its wheels. The same trick pushes a grounded boat back into the water."
  - q: "How do you swim faster in GTA 6?"
    a: "Click the left stick once for a faster stroke and twice for full speed. Diving switches you from stamina to lung capacity, which lets you keep that speed up for longer."
