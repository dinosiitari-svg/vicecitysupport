question: "How to get the key cloner in GTA 6"
answer: "You get the key cloner in Chapter 3. After Crosstown Traffic and a few more story missions, Randy phones and starts the mission to steal a Zorrusso for him (GTABase and Vice call it Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs). During it you install the key cloner on your phone and use it on a red Zorrusso in an Ocean Beach parking garage. You can't get it any earlier and you can't miss it. To use it, stand at a keyless car's driver door, choose Clone Key and turn the right stick until the meter fills."

  - title: "Play the Chapter 3 story to Crosstown Traffic"
    detail: "You first meet Randy at the end of the Chapter 3 mission where you steal a car for Raul. Keep going through Crosstown Traffic. Guides list different missions after it (Rare Thief also had Credit Check, Scope Out the Bank, Security Expert and Ride or Die), so just keep doing the story."
  - title: "Wait for Randy to call"
    detail: "The mission only appears once Randy phones you, and you can't trigger the call yourself. Rare Thief got it about two in-game days after Crosstown Traffic. Sleeping at a safehouse passes time between jobs."
  - title: "Install the key cloner on your phone"
    detail: "When prompted, open the phone, go to Settings > Third-Party Plugins and install key_cloner_v1.6.9."
  - title: "Find the Zorrusso in Ocean Beach"
    detail: "It's a red Zorrusso in the covered parking at the Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach, behind a red gate and a white wall you can climb over."
  - title: "Clone the key"
    detail: "Go to the driver's door, press the Clone Key prompt (Y on Xbox per Rare Thief), then turn the right stick until the meter turns pink and hold it there until it fills."
  - title: "Drive it to Randy without slowing down"
    detail: "The Zorrusso has an advanced tracker, so police are alerted if you drive slowly. Rare Thief says the signal is blocked near Leonida Auto World, so you can ease off for the last stretch. Damage lowers Randy's payment (Rare Thief got about $20,000)."
  - title: "Use it on any keyless car"
    detail: "Scan a car with WAiNK first. If the access type says Key Cloner, Clone Key replaces the Slim Jim option at its door. Most high-end sports cars and luxury EVs are keyless."
