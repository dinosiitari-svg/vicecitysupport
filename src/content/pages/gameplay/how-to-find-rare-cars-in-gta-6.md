Randy's Wish List opens in Chapter 3 after the mission Steal the Zorrusso for Randy: it's a list of high-end cars Randy wants, tracked under Robberies on your Touch Pad, and he pays extra for one in good condition. ## Tracker levels at a glance

| Tracker | What it means (as reported) |
|---|---|
| None | Moses will buy or register it, but not if you took it near his shop |
| Standard | Police learn where it was stolen; Randy or a Pay 'n' Spray will take it |
| Advanced | Usually supercars; police are called if you drive too slowly; Randy only |
