question: "How to fast travel in GTA 6"
answer: "GTA 6 has no 'click the map and teleport' fast travel. Instead you take a ride and pay to skip it. Paradise Taxi (a phone contact) opens after the first Chapter 1 mission, Gone Fishing, and is paid with the cash you carry. The RydeMe app arrives once you have a bank account from the Buck Me mission and charges your account. Either way, pressing Triangle / Y during the ride skips the trip for about $100 on top of the fare."

## What isn't confirmed yet

- **Is Paradise Taxi real fast travel?** VGC calls RydeMe the only proper fast travel and doesn't mention Paradise Taxi at all. GTABase and KeenGamer both describe Paradise Taxi as the earlier option.
- **When RydeMe appears.** Most guides say right after Buck Me; KeenGamer says some players only get it in Chapter 2.
- **Paying to speed up vs skipping.** VGC describes paying the driver to go faster as separate from skipping; the other guides treat it as the same ~$100 skip.
- Whether a wanted level or an active mission blocks booking a ride. No guide mentions it yet.
- Whether planes or airports offer any fast travel. None of the guides mention it.
