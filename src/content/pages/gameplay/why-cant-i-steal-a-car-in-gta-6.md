items:
  - title: "The car is keyless"
    detail: "Newer cars with key fobs can't be hotwired. Smashing the window gets you in, but the engine won't start. Fix: get the key cloner in Chapter 3 (mission \"Steal the Zorruso for Randy\"), stand by the driver's door, press interact and choose Clone Key."
  - title: "You don't have the slim jim yet"
    detail: "Before Chapter 2, smashing the window is the only way into a locked parked car. Moses gives you the slim jim in the Chapter 2 mission \"Moses\". On PS5, Triangle uses the slim jim and Circle smashes the window."
  - title: "You don't know what the car needs"
    detail: "Hold up on the D-pad to scan it with the WAINK app. It shows whether it's locked, has an alarm or a tracker, and roughly what it's worth. If you don't have the right tool, pick a different car; trying again won't help."
  - title: "The owner fights back"
    detail: "When you carjack, some drivers resist or pull a gun, and owners can report the theft to the police. Pick a different target or get away fast."
  - title: "Police keep finding you"
    detail: "That's probably a tracker. Expensive cars often have one that sends your position to the police. Keep your speed up until they lose the signal, or swap cars. A fence can remove the tracker later."
  - title: "Quickest fix: carjack"
    detail: "You can pull a driver out of a moving car at any point in the story, and the engine is already running, so no tools are needed."
