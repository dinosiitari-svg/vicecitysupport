## Before you go

Several of these spots are reached by boat, and the Kingfisher Compound is guarded, so bring something to fight with. Free guns are handy before you can afford the [gun stores](/gameplay/where-to-buy-guns-in-gta-6/), but buy your favourites properly once you can: owned weapons survive death, picked-up ones don't.

None of these spots are on our map yet because the guides describe them in words only. We'll pin them once we've found them in-game.
