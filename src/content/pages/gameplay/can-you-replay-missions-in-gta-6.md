## What isn't confirmed yet

- Whether Rockstar will add a replay option in a later update.
- Whether any single mission can be retried from a mid-mission checkpoint after you fail it (reports so far don't cover this).
- Sources disagree on wording: Dexerto calls loading an old save "replaying", while Engadget and VGC say there's no replay at all. In practice they describe the same thing.
