## Which method should you use?

| Method | Works on | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| Smash window, then hotwire | Older cars | Loud, can trigger the alarm and witnesses |
| Slim jim, then hotwire | Older cars (from Chapter 2) | Takes a few seconds in plain view |
| Key cloner | Keyless newer cars (from Chapter 3) | Not available early on |
| Carjack | Any occupied car | Driver may fight back or report you |
