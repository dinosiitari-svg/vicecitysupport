## What isn't confirmed yet

- **The exact station count.** Guides say 22 or 23 depending on whether The Vault Radio counts as a station or a hidden playlist.
- **Genre labels.** VGC and KeenGamer describe several stations differently (VCRR as trap or hip-hop, Kaleidoscope FM as synth pop or international pop). We've combined both.
- **The 810-song total.** It comes from a single guide.
- **Custom playlists.** No guide mentions a GTA V-style Self Radio or a way to build your own playlist.
