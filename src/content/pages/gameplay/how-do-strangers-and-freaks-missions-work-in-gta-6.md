## Questlines by chapter

| Chapter | Strangers | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Dr. Merle, Freddie, Dodos Rising | Both |
| 1 | Shirley (missable), Kenton, Cal, Kirk | Both; Kirk's finale is Lucia only |
| 2 | Kyle, Alvan, Moses, Bret Vogel, Billy | Bret is Jason only |
| 3 | Yago, Andy, Yasmani, Randy, Tyrone, Dunk, Papa Audacity | Andy is Lucia only; Yago's later races Jason only |
| 4 | Eddie Acosta, Pilot Pete, Shayna | Pete is Jason only; Shayna is Lucia only |

## Trophies tied to side missions

Besides Strangers & Freaks itself, both guides name Fair Game (Bret Vogel), The Lone Rider (Billy), Road to Rehabilitation (Andy), Spin On This! (Tyrone) and Supply & Demand (Randy). KeenGamer also links This Was No Boat Accident to Kenton's dive sites, Baited My Tackle to Cal's fishing, and The Truth Is Out There to the UFO questline.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The exact eight questlines the trophy counts (guides disagree on Alvan and Papa Audacity).
- Whether Cal's fishing strand counts (PowerPyx says no).
- When Dodos Rising first appears (any chapter vs Chapter 3).
- Cash rewards for most questlines.
