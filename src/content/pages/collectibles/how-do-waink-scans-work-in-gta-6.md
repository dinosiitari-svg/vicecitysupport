## The four WAiNK groups

| Group | Reported count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Landmarks | 31 | Well-known buildings and sights around Leonida |
| Curiosities | 35 | Odd or unusual objects out in the world |
| Sculptures | 23 | Statues and decorative installations along streets and parks |
| No Flusher tags | 23 | Graffiti-style tags; one guide lists them under WAiNK, another only counts them |

Landmarks reportedly cluster in Mariana County, Kelly County and Vice City, so a drive through those areas ticks off a good share early.

## What isn't confirmed yet

The exact number of No Flusher tags, whether a fifth group exists (one guide says the menu shows five), how close you need to be to trigger the rumble, and whether both Jason and Lucia's scans count toward the same total. We'll update this page as we check.
