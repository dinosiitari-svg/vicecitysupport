## Tips

- All four Sniper of La Perle posters are close together, so you can do them in one trip. La Perle's three shops are a short walk apart, and Jack of Hearts is nearby in Crosstown.
- Jack of Hearts may not be open until Chapter 3, so save that poster for later if you can't get in.
- The Vault poster is right inside the door, so it takes seconds once you're Downtown.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The exact spot of each poster inside its building. Our map pins are placed at the building, so look around inside.
- Whether any poster is locked behind a story chapter.
- Whether either playlist is tied to a trophy.
