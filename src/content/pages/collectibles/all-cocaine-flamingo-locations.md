## Tips

- Belpoint is the best place to start: 6 flamingos are close together there.
- The Featherstone Islands off north-west Lummox County have 6 more; take a boat or helicopter and clear them in one trip.
- Some flamingos stand in groups of decorations. Only the ones with a black-and-white beak break, so use Focus to spot the real one.
- They can be picked up any time in free roam, so there's no rush during the story.
