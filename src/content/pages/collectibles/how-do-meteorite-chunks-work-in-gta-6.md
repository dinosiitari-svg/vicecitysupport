## Where players are finding them

Chunks are reported all over Leonida, often near the coast. Places named in launch-week guides include a Belville garage, under a pier on Belpoint Island, the Sandbars, islands in Leonida Bay, and the underwater caves at Selkie Hole. A few more are near a campsite crater and close to the summit of the big northern mountain. All of these are described in words, not coordinates, so we haven't put them on our map yet.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- The total number of chunks. Guides say anything from 26 found so far to more than 70.
- Who buys them and for how much: Kenton, the Lifeinvader marketplace or a pawn shop.
- Whether finding all of them gives you anything extra.
- Where picked-up chunks show up in your inventory.

We'll update this page as we check them in-game.
