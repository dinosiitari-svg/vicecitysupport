## PC version rumours

On 22 November 2026 reports picked up a GamesBeat post citing unnamed inside sources, which says Rockstar plans to release GTA 6 on PC in the first three months of 2027. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed this, so treat it as a rumour. Some headlines called it an "official" update; it isn't.

## Unofficial PC ports

A week after launch, hobbyist projects built on a compatibility layer for the Xbox version (reported as "VIctory") got GTA 6's open world rendering on PC, but only at three to four frames per second with a few missions completable. These are not a PC release. Take-Two has reportedly had around 3,000 related GitHub repositories removed, and Rockstar still hasn't announced an official PC version. Treat any "GTA 6 PC download" as a scam or malware risk.

Since then the projects have been shutting down one by one:

- **24 November:** Take-Two filed a takedown against a fork network of about 3,007 repositories. The lead of one project, "Project Leonida", said he was stepping away and claimed Rockstar had the team's Discord server removed. GamesRadar+ reports that as his claim, not a confirmed fact.
- **26 November:** a port tracker claimed another project was hitting around 20–25 fps on a high-end Nvidia card. The developer's own test logs showed closer to 17–18 fps. The code was public on GitHub for under an hour before it became inaccessible, and it's not known whether it was deleted, made private or blocked.

Rockstar and Take-Two haven't commented on any of this. PC Games Hardware also warns that fake tracker sites and "finished port" downloads may carry malware.

We'll update this page immediately if Rockstar announces a PC version or any other platform.
