## Why this is a big deal

Only one game had ever reached 99 on Metacritic before, and it did so in 1998. Plenty of acclaimed games since then have landed on 97 or 98, including Rockstar's own GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. GTA 6 is the first in nearly three decades to reach the top.
